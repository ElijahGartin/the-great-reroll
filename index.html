HOW TO PLAY

The whole draft in five steps.
1

Set Up the Draft

Choose Horde or Alliance, add your players, and choose which race/class combinations are eligible.

2

Roll for Draft Order

Everyone rolls a d100. Highest roll picks first. Ties reroll.

3

Get Your Choices

On your turn, you get the number of random race/class options set in the draft settings. Click one to select it—you can still change your mind before locking it in.

4

Lock It In

Hit LOCK IT IN to make your pick official. That exact combination is removed so nobody else can draft it.

5

Next Player

Move down the draft order until everyone has a Forever character.

EXTRA SPIN: Every player gets one optional re-spin. It generates the same number of options you configured for each turn (1 option = 1 new roll, 3 options = 3 new rolls, etc.). The re-spin draws from the same eligible pool, so a previous option can appear again—and there is even a small chance of getting the exact same set. Once you re-spin, you must choose from the newly generated set.
